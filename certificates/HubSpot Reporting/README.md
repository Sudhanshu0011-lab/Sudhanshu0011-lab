## HubSpot Reporting Certification: Skills Gained

Coursework covering CRM data structure, dashboard design, traffic and funnel analytics,
attribution, customer journey analysis, and custom report building.

### CRM Data Modeling and Data Quality
- Mapped CRM data using relational database concepts (objects as tables, records as rows, properties as columns, associations as relationships).
- Used the data model to explain why records appear or drop out of cross-object reports.
- Selected property types (number, date, drop-down, multiple checkbox, score, calculation) based on filtering and reporting needs.
- Handled null values deliberately, including when to backfill and when to leave them blank so averages are not distorted.
- Applied data-cleansing principles: accurate, complete, consistent, uniform and accessible.
- Worked with first-party data, consent and data-protection practices (GDPR/CCPA awareness).
- Distinguished mutable records from immutable events, and applied custom behavioral events to intent segmentation.

### Dashboard Design and Stakeholder Reporting
- Designed dashboards by working backwards from a business question and the viewer's role.
- Applied visual hierarchy: chart selection, colour coding and report placement (high-level first, then drill-downs).
- Added annotations, embedded content and training material so dashboards can be read without help.
- Managed dashboard permissions, cloning, activity logs and sharing (scheduled email, link, Slack, export).
- Built example dashboards: marketing value, sales-marketing SLA, rep KPI tracker and sales forecast hygiene.

### Web, Marketing, Sales and Service Analytics
- Analyzed traffic by source, page, topic cluster, UTM parameter, country, device and browser.
- Used trend, outlier and conversion-rate approaches to diagnose channel performance and set benchmarks from historical data.
- Built consistent UTM naming conventions, and avoided UTM use on internal links to prevent inflated metrics.
- Evaluated forms, ads, social, email and campaigns against SMART goals and ROI.
- Tracked flywheel KPIs: conversion rate, win rate, sales cycle length, NPS, CSAT and referral rate.
- Calculated customer acquisition cost (CAC), customer lifetime value (LTV) and the LTV:CAC ratio, and interpreted the 3:1 benchmark.
- Interpreted sales and service analytics: quota attainment, deal push rate, ticket time to close, chat wait time and support volume.

### Attribution and Customer Journey Analytics
- Compared first-touch, last-touch, linear, time-decay, U-, W-, J-shaped and full-path attribution models and matched each to a business question.
- Built contact-create, deal-create and revenue attribution reports using dimensions, filters and a consistent model for period-over-period comparison.
- Built customer journey (Sankey) reports with branching and optional steps to find conversion drop-offs.
- Used journey analytics and attribution together to avoid false positives, such as high revenue driven by volume rather than conversion.
- Segmented drop-off lists for re-engagement campaigns.

### Custom Reporting and Analytical Method
- Framed each report with a clear business question and intended action before building it, moving from data to insight to decision.
- Chose primary and secondary data sources, and predicted join results (HubSpot uses left joins).
- Configured measures and dimensions, applied aggregations (sum, average, min, max, distinct count), and set sorting and limits.
- Wrote filter logic with AND, OR and AND NOT.
- Built time-series charts with goal lines and log-scale handling, and created datasets with calculated fields (such as MRR tiering).
- Communicated findings and next steps to non-technical stakeholders.
