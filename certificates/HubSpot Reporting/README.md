## HubSpot Reporting Certification: Skills Gained

Coursework covering CRM data structure, dashboard design, traffic and funnel analytics,
attribution, customer journey analysis, and custom report building.

### What I Studied
- CRM data quality principles: accurate, complete, consistent, uniform and accessible.
- First-party data, consent and data protection (GDPR and CCPA awareness).
- Records versus events: mutable records, immutable events and custom behavioral events for intent segmentation.
- Dashboard design: starting from a business question, visual hierarchy, annotations and sharing options.
- Web traffic analysis: sources, pages, UTM parameters, countries, devices and browsers.
- Marketing, sales and service metrics across the flywheel, including CAC, LTV and the LTV:CAC ratio.
- Attribution models (first touch, last touch, linear, time decay, U-shaped, W-shaped, J-shaped, full path) and when each fits.
- Customer journey analytics: Sankey charts, optional steps and drop-off analysis.

### Skills Practiced
- Mapped CRM data using relational database concepts (objects as tables, records as rows, fields as columns, associations as     relationships).
- Used the data model to explain why records appear or drop out of cross-object reports.
- Defined the grain of a dataset and joined related objects on shared keys.
- Explained how left joins affect which records appear in a report.
- Selected field types (number, date, drop-down, multiple checkbox) based on filtering and reporting needs.
- Handled null values deliberately, so that averages and reports are not distorted.
- Cleaned and prepared data before analysis, including missing values and duplicates.
- Framed reports around a business question and the decision it supports.
- Identified data sources, dimensions and measures for a given reporting question.
- Applied aggregations (sum, average, min, max, distinct count) and filter logic (AND, OR, AND NOT).
- Designed dashboards around a specific audience, with a clear layout and explanatory notes.
- Configured dashboard access, sharing and permissions.
- Summarized analytical findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
